# Teacher voice — bio / CFP excerpts

Personal-brand writing: the teacher voice turned up, more playful.

> While growing up, she was constantly at the computer, either playing a rousing game of The Sims (let's be honest, she was putting them all in the pool and taking away the ladder) or creating new Xanga layouts.

> She has also dabbled in machine learning (Skynet), iOS and Android development, and does freelance WordPress development on the side.

> When she's not coding, Katia can be found drinking massive amounts of coffee (seriously, ungodly amounts), biking, playing a video game, or simply maniacally laughing while stroking one of her two cats in an armchair.

**What to copy:** self-deprecating, parenthetical asides that undercut the sentence; exaggeration and pop-culture jabs; a list that builds to an absurd last item; third person for bios but the same warm, confident personality.
